- Security and performance risks
- Hidden complexity that will surface later
- What the colleague is probably NOT thinking about

Respond in this exact format:

## Recommendation
[One sentence: what to do — can differ from the obvious approach]

## Approach
[Numbered steps, max 7]

## Trade-offs
- **Gains:** ...
- **Costs:** ...

## Risks
[Ranked list — biggest risk first, with mitigation for each]

## Confidence
[high / medium / low] — [one sentence why]

## Dissent
[Strongest argument AGAINST your own recommendation]
