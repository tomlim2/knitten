# Knitten Sauvignon Blanc

**Brand:** Citizen Wine
**Region:** Marlborough, New Zealand
**Label motif:** Vintage pen-and-ink tabby cat knitting with yarn and needles
**Typography:** Classical transitional serif all-caps "KNITTEN" + italic script "Sauvignon Blanc" — vintage letterpress apothecary / old European bookplate vibe

## Palette (3-color strict)
- Sage mint `#A8C4B0` — background, foliage, walls
- Deep ink black `#1A1A1A` — line work, outlines, cat stripes, signage
- Warm cream `#F2EBDB` — highlights, rooftops, sails, wine, cobblestones

## Regional research
- **Landmark chosen:** Edwin Fox historic wooden tall ship (Picton dry-dock) — one of the world's oldest surviving wooden ships
- **Specialties:** Greenshell mussels (Havelock = world capital), Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc grapes, kaimoana Māori seafood tradition
- **Geography:** Marlborough Sounds fjord inlets, Wairau Valley terraced vineyards
- **Flora/fauna:** silver fern, tui birds, fantail, sheep on hillsides
